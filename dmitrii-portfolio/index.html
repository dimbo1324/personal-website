02

Case Studies.

Three projects, each as problem → approach → result.

02.A Transneft A&M · 2023 – 2025

Reusable PLC library

−25% delivery time

Problem
Every new PLC project rebuilt standard logic from scratch, and one delivery took four weeks.
Approach
Created a reusable IEC 61131-3 function-block library and a no-code FBD DSL on top of it, then used it in SAT/FAT at three oil pumping stations.
Result
Delivery went from 4 to 3 weeks. SAT/FAT at the three stations covered Siemens, Schneider, B&R and Prosoft equipment.
  • IEC 61131-3
  • FBD DSL
  • Siemens / Schneider
  • B&R
02.B LUKOIL · Kstovo refinery · 2025 – 2026

Live refinery commissioning

2,500+ field I/O signals

Problem
Units L-24/7 and AVT-5 had to be commissioned while connected to a live safety system.
Approach
Ran full-cycle SAT/FAT with live SIS/ESD validation on Prosoft / Elara controllers.
Result
More than 2,500 field I/O signals verified and the SIS/ESD logic validated on the running units.
  • SAT / FAT
  • SIS / ESD
  • Prosoft / Elara
  • MQTT / OPC UA
02.C NIITOP · 2021 – 2022

Approach
Automated the line, and also built an ATmega48 cooling controller for a 5 kW microwave load.
Result
The operator no longer works in the toxic zone, and line yield went up 12%.
